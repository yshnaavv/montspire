University Admissions & Requirements

Several universities in the UK, USA, Australia, and Germany accept alternative proof of English proficiency such as Duolingo English Test (DET), MOI (Medium of Instruction certificate from your previous university), or high school English marks (usually 70%+). Our counselors can shortlist these institutions for you.

The primary intakes are:
  • Fall Intake (August / September): The largest intake with maximum course availability and scholarship options.
  • Spring Intake (January / February): The secondary intake preferred by students who missed Fall deadlines.
  • Summer Intake (May / June): Available for specific diploma & short-term programs.

Student Visa & Financial Guidance

Financial requirements vary by country. Generally, you need to show proof of 1 year’s tuition fee plus living expenses:
  • USA (F-1 Visa): Equivalent to 1 year I-20 estimate (~$35,000 - $55,000 USD).
  • UK (Student Visa): Tuition fee + £1,334/month living cost (inner London) or £1,023/month (outer London) for 9 months.
  • Germany (Blocked Account): €11,208 per year deposited into an official German blocked account.

Work Rights & Post-Graduation Career

Yes! Most destinations allow international students to work up to 20 hours per week during academic semesters and full-time (40 hours per week) during official semester breaks.

Post-study work rights differ by country:
  • USA: 1 year OPT (up to 3 years for STEM graduates).
  • UK: 2 years Graduate Route visa.
  • Canada: 1 to 3 years Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP).
  • Australia: 2 to 4 years Subclass 485 visa depending on qualification and location.
